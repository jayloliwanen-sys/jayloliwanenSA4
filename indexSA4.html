About Our School

Immaculate Heart High School is a school located in Natonin, Mountain Province. It is a place where students learn important knowledge, skills, and values that can help them in the future.

Our school provides a friendly learning environment where students are encouraged to study hard, respect one another, and participate in different school activities.

Our School Values

Immaculate Heart High School values faith, respect, responsibility, honesty, and cooperation. These values guide students to become better learners and responsible members of the community.

Our Goal

Our goal is to help students develop their talents, knowledge, and character so they can face the future with confidence and become responsible citizens.
